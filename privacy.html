Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the Unbox app for Android (package uk.jj.unbox) and this website. Unbox is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Unbox does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Your list of apps, your backups and your settings stay on your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Unbox keeps your settings, favourites, watch list, saved filters, the list of backups, tracker results and a copy of your app list (for a quick start) in the app's private storage. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for Unbox. The files you extract are saved in Download/Unbox, or in a folder you choose, and are yours to keep, move or delete.

Lists you export

CSV and PDF lists of your apps are made only when you ask, and saved where you choose. Once saved, they are yours to keep, share or delete.

Links to this website

Settings, About has Check for updates, Website and More apps. Each opens this site in your browser; Unbox itself never goes online.

Keeping and deleting your data

Settings, Reset Unbox clears everything Unbox keeps, apart from the files you saved, which stay where they are. Uninstalling Unbox deletes everything in its private storage.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. The only script on it unlocks the download once the lawful-use box is ticked. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 26 November 2026.
