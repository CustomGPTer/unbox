What Unbox does not do

Unbox saves apps, not the data inside them, and it cannot uninstall system apps or clear other apps' data. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the others do more

ML Manager can upload APKs to APKMirror and, in its Pro version with root, hide apps and clear their cache. Both install and update from Google Play.
